MobilesWorld Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 18 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the MobilesWorld Android application (package name com.pakistan.mobilesworld) handles information when you use the app.

1. Overview

MobilesWorld is a mobile-device catalogue and comparison app. It does not require an account and does not ask you to provide a name, phone number, contact list, precise location, or payment information.

The app stores your catalogue preferences locally. It also uses third-party services for app configuration, diagnostics, advertising, update checks, content delivery, and—when configured and permitted—push notifications.

2. Information handled by the app

Information stored on your device

The following information may be stored locally:

  • saved or favourite devices;
  • device comparison selections;
  • recent searches and search history;
  • theme and display preferences; and
  • cached catalogue pages, specifications, and images.

This information is not intentionally uploaded to the developer. Android backup or device-transfer features may copy eligible local app data when those system features are enabled.

Information processed automatically

Depending on the enabled feature and your choices, service providers may process:

  • IP address and IP-derived general location;
  • device model, operating-system version, app version, language, and technical identifiers;
  • app-instance, installation, diagnostic, crash, and performance information;
  • feature usage and interactions with advertisements;
  • the Android advertising ID, when available; and
  • a push-subscription identifier and notification delivery or interaction events.

Information you send to us

If you contact support, we receive the email address and information you choose to include in your message. Do not send sensitive personal information that is not needed to answer your request.

3. How information is used

Information is used as necessary to:

  • retrieve and display device specifications, product images, and brand assets;
  • remember your local preferences and provide offline or cached content;
  • deliver compatible app configuration and check for required updates;
  • detect failures, protect service integrity, and improve reliability;
  • request, display, measure, and prevent fraud in advertising;
  • manage privacy and advertising consent choices; and
  • deliver and measure push notifications if notifications are enabled.

4. Third-party services

MobilesWorld may communicate with the following providers. Their processing is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

  • Google Firebase: Remote Config, Analytics, Crashlytics, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for configuration, diagnostics, analytics, and push delivery. See Firebase Privacy and Security.
  • Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform: advertisements, measurement, fraud prevention, and applicable consent choices. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play: application distribution, update availability, and in-app update handling.
  • OneSignal: push-subscription management and notification delivery when OneSignal is configured and notifications are enabled. See OneSignal's Privacy Policy.
  • Catalogue and image providers: public GSMArena pages and external image, favicon, or brand-logo hosts receive ordinary network request information when the app retrieves content.

This policy page is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary web request and diagnostic information when you visit this page.

5. Advertising and consent

Advertising can be remotely enabled or disabled. When enabled, Google Mobile Ads and its advertising partners may use device information, IP-derived general location, advertising identifiers, diagnostics, and ad interactions to provide, measure, and protect advertisements. Native and interstitial advertisements are identified in the app.

Where required, the app presents Google's consent choices before requesting ads. You can revisit available privacy choices from the app, and you can reset or delete the Android advertising ID in Android settings. The ads shown may depend on your region, consent status, device settings, and provider configuration.

6. Push notifications

On supported Android versions, the app requests notification permission. You may deny the request without losing access to the catalogue. If push is configured and you allow notifications, OneSignal and Firebase Cloud Messaging may process an app or push subscription identifier, device and app technical information, IP address, and delivery or interaction events.

You can disable notifications at any time in Android's notification settings for MobilesWorld.

7. Android permissions

  • Internet and network state: used to retrieve catalogue content, configuration, images, advertisements, updates, and notifications.
  • Notifications: used only to ask whether the app may display push notifications on supported Android versions.
  • Advertising ID: may be used by the advertising SDK for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, subject to platform rules and your choices.

The current app does not request access to contacts, the microphone, the camera, precise location, or shared photos and files.

8. Sharing, sale, and retention

The developer does not sell your saved devices, comparisons, or search history. Information is shared with service providers only as described in this policy or when required by law.

Local information remains until you remove it in the app, clear app storage, or uninstall the app, subject to any Android backup copy. Third-party providers retain information according to their own policies, legal obligations, and configured retention periods. Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably needed to respond, maintain records, prevent abuse, or comply with law.

9. Your choices and rights

You can:

  • remove favourites, comparison entries, and recent searches in the app;
  • clear all local data through Android's app-storage settings;
  • deny or disable notifications through Android settings;
  • use available in-app advertising privacy choices;
  • reset or delete the Android advertising ID; and
  • control Android backup and device-transfer features.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection concerning personal information handled by the developer. Contact us to make a request. We may need to verify the request, and some provider-controlled information must be managed directly through that provider.

10. Security and international processing

The app uses HTTPS for network requests and limits its requested Android permissions. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their safeguards and applicable law.

11. Children's privacy

MobilesWorld is a general-audience device-information app and is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so it can be reviewed and deleted where applicable.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app, its providers, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date. Material changes may also be communicated in the app or through the store listing when appropriate.

13. Contact us

For questions, privacy requests, or concerns, email support@mobilesworld.com.pk.
